What God will not tolerate

God will not bless a practice that trains the body and the imagination that pain, humiliation, and domination are the path to closeness. That is a lie about love. He will not treat “consent” as a substitute for covenant, nor “exploration” as a substitute for holiness. He will not share His altar with a master that uses His image as a canvas for harm.

He will not tolerate the objectifying of women or men — misogyny and misandry dressed as analysis, ideology, or erotic taste. Hatred of one sex does not “balance” hatred of the other. Both tear the image of God in male and female, poison marriage and parenting, and justify using people.

He will not tolerate wicked imaginations as a private hobby. The mind is the rehearsal hall. What is rehearsed as domination, humiliation, or contempt becomes the life. Proverbs 6:18 names a heart that devises wicked imaginations. 2 Corinthians 10:4–5 requires every thought taken captive to Christ, not entertained as identity.

Romans 1 — a mind given over: worship of the creature, then uncleanness, then a debased mind that approves what should shame.
Romans 12:2 — be transformed by the renewing of the mind.
Philippians 4:8 — think on what is true, pure, and honorable.
Galatians 5 — the fruit of the Spirit does not grow in a dungeon of the mind.

Social building vs destruction

A people is built when men and women treat one another as image-bearers, keep covenant, protect the weak, and raise children in safety. Sadism and masochism invert that: one party is used as a canvas for harm; the other learns to need harm to feel alive. Even when both “agree,” the agreement is still a lie about what love is. The map names the root deception for that reason.

A culture can look energized while this is happening (clubs, content, ideology, “empowerment” language). That is not social building. It is social consumption: people, families, and the next generation are spent for sensation, revenge, or status. Between BDSM, misogyny, and misandry: they promote destruction. They construct networks of use, not a stable people.

Who “survives” this life, and who does not

“Survive” has two meanings. Do not confuse them.

In the flesh’s economy

People who harden last longer on the surface: the one who can dominate without conscience, the one who can eroticize their own wound until it feels like identity, the one who can hate a whole sex and still function at work. They look like survivors because they have killed tenderness. Sensitivity, covenant loyalty, and a clean conscience become liabilities in that world. People who still have a living conscience, who still need real love, who cannot treat a human as a prop — they burn out, break, or leave. The practices select for the already wounded and the already cruel, then deepen both.

In God’s economy

That “survival” is death walking. The people who truly live are those who repent and come out — not those who become the best at the game. The ones who stay in it do not “win the culture”; they lose the soul, and they often lose the body, the marriage, and the children along the way.

Children, the traumatized, and anyone still trying to be loved as a person cannot thrive there, because the system requires a false self: either the one who inflicts, the one who absorbs, or the one who hates. God’s image in a person is not designed to live as predator, prey, or partisan of contempt.

Why it exists

It exists because deception offers a counterfeit for three things God actually made:

  1. Power — dominion was given to steward creation, not to crush a neighbor’s will (see #379 immediately after this topic).
  2. Intimacy — covenant love, not fused pain.
  3. Meaning after wounding — healing and justice, not rehearsing the wound as pleasure or ideology.

The map’s recognize-line is blunt: this fruit is Sexual Corruption under Sexual Perversion, held by agreements, blood language, and ritual-style binding — not by “healthy exploration.” Wicked imagination is the training ground before the act: the mind rehearses domination, humiliation, or hatred until the body follows. Misogyny and misandry often feed that imagination (the other sex as enemy or toy). BDSM often enacts it. They are one family of lie: the image of God is something you use.

It also exists because trauma, pornography, occult imitation, and unforgiveness create a hunger that counterfeit fire can fill for a season. The fire is real as sensation; it is false as life.

Mental training to stop walking in it

This is not “think positive.” It is renewing the mind until the old pictures lose jurisdiction (Romans 12:2; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5).

  1. Name the root. Stop calling it identity, art, or justice. Call it deception and falsehood, and call objectifying men or women what the map calls it: lust and lewdness, not “analysis.”
  2. Take every imagination captive. When the scene, the hatred, or the humiliation-script starts, do not negotiate with it. Refuse it, replace it with what is true, pure, and honorable (Philippians 4:8). Starving the rehearsal starves the practice.
  3. Forgive and take responsibility. The compiled prayer for #378 refuses blaming the teachers of the habit, takes responsibility for what was copied, and asks the Blood to cover the record. Hatred of a sex is often unhealed injury wearing a theory. Forgiveness does not deny harm; it stops serving the harm as master.
  4. Retrain how you see bodies. Practice seeing male and female as image of God, not as objects, enemies, or fuel. That is the opposite of #374 and #376. If you cannot honor a person without a power-script, you are not yet free; you are only between scenes.
  5. Break agreement, not just behavior. Come off the altar of that master. Ask God to divorce the record, close the doors of pornography, ritual, and fantasy, and fill the vacancy with the Holy Spirit — or the old hunger returns with seven worse pictures.
  6. Rebuild ordinary covenant. Faithfulness, self-control, kindness, and protecting the weak are the replacement training. Galatians 5’s fruit grows where you tell the truth, keep your body, and refuse to weaponize another person’s shame.

Summary. God will not tolerate BDSM because it is deception about love, power, and the image of God. He will not tolerate misogyny or misandry as the fuel for that deception. What looks like survival in that world is hardness. What looks like dying on the way out is often the false self losing jurisdiction. People who will not do the mind-work can still function. They will not become a people. People who do that work often feel like they are dying at first — because the false self is dying. That death is the only kind that leads to a life that can actually build a home, a church, or a nation.
