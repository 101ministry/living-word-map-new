BIBLE TEACHERS — GOVERNMENT OBEDIENCE COMPARISON
Living Word Map / Deportation Study appendix
Compiled: 2026-08-02

QUESTION
  A) Does the teacher treat Romans 13 (and parallels) as binding duty to the
     CURRENT civil government where you live (taxes, laws, honor officials),
     with a narrow Acts 4:19 / Acts 5:29 exception when the state forbids what
     God commands or commands what God forbids?

  B) Does the teacher frame "obeying government" mainly as obedience to God
     APART FROM the physical magistrate — i.e. heavenly citizenship or
     spiritual authority displaces civil submission?

SUMMARY COUNTS (9 teachers listed by user)
  Category A (current civil government):     6 of 9
  Category B (God apart from physical state): 0 of 9
  Unclear / not publicly taught on record:    3 of 9

NOTE ON OVERLAP
  All six Category A teachers also say God outranks the state when the two
  directly conflict. That is standard evangelical dual citizenship, NOT
  Category B.

CATEGORY DEFINITIONS (for cross-study use)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A — CURRENT CIVIL GOVERNMENT
      Default: obey the magistrate you are under (laws, taxes, respect).
      Exception: obey God rather than men when direct conflict (Acts 4:19).
      Passages usually cited: Rom 13:1–7, 1 Pet 2:13–17, Titus 3:1, Matt 22:21.

  B — GOD APART FROM PHYSICAL STATE
      "Government obedience" collapsed into spiritual obedience only; little or
      no binding duty to civil authority; or explicit "Caesar does not apply."
      None of the nine teachers clearly teach this on record.

  ? — UNCLEAR
      Insufficient public teaching on Rom 13:1–7 / civil magistrate duty to
      classify as A or B.
