THE POWER OF VIBRATING FOR EVIL

Ephesians 6:12, corporate word-agreement, election, and the twelve-gate zodiac covenant

Subject: How spoken words — slogans, oaths, receptions, and collective consent — align a person or a people with spiritual powers named in Scripture, mapped in the zodiac deliverance tradition, and repeated in modern election of rulers.

Key text: Ephesians 6:12 (Greek NT). Zodiac structure from deliverance renunciation prayers (twelve signs, deny/receive pattern).

Claim: To vibrate (speak, declare, chant, vote, agree) for evil is not neutral speech. It installs lordship at the gates of the soul and body — and passes down through generations until renounced in Messiah Y’shua.

I. The Greek — Ephesians 6:12

For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of the darkness of this present age, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.

Paul does not deny that flesh-and-blood rulers exist. He denies that they are the deepest enemy. Human government, cultural slogans, and generational habits can become channels for ἀρχαί and κοσμοκράτορες when a people speak agreement with their order.

II. The power of vibrating (speaking) for evil

In deliverance theology and in Scripture, words are not empty noise. They bind, release, covenant, and transfer lordship.

  • Proverbs 18:21 — Death and life are in the power of the tongue.
  • Matthew 12:37 — By your words you will be justified, and by your words you will be condemned.
  • 1 Samuel 8:7 — Israel asked for a king; God said they rejected Him that He should not reign over them.
  • Joshua 24:15 — Choose this day whom you will serve.

To vibrate for evil is to speak, repeat, or corporately consent to a slogan, identity, or ruler that is not Messiah — until that speech becomes occupancy at the gates of the person: mind, speech, heart, womb, knees, walk.

V. Deny and receive — the two covenants

Each deliverance prayer follows one logic:

1. Reception (enslavement)

Implicit in the sign: receive false courage, wealth, identity, destiny, insight, control — through slogan and ruler.

2. Renunciation (liberation)

I deny… I cut myself loose… I renounce… — revoking prior word-agreement with the sign, element, and ruler.

3. Lawful reception (freedom)

In the Name of Messiah Y’shua, I possess ALL my GATES with VICTORY. Legal right to receive from the Father — life, wisdom, sonship — only under His lordship.

Scorpio’s prayer names generational transmission explicitly: powers of death possibly over generations of my ancestors. Election and zodiac both work corporately — the child may never vote, yet inherits the structure.

VI. Three signs that expose the alliance

Aries — PRINCE

I AM, I WILL, I PREPARE THE WAY

Anti-Messiah speech at the gate of the head. A ruler elected to “prepare the nation’s way” on human terms repeats Aries at scale: a prince replaces the Prince of Peace. Meaning in the prayer: PRINCE.

Leo — SUN (THE GREAT BABYLON)

I CREATE, I AM THE CENTRE

Worship shifted to the state, the leader, or the age. Babylon is the solar centre at the heart — beast-worship in personal and national form.

Capricorn — I MASTER, I STRUCTURE, I ORGANISE

I MASTER, I STRUCTURE, I CORDON OFF, I ORGANISE

The electoral act in pure form: choose the system, accept the cordon, live inside the structure. Winter solstice / gate of the gods — calendar lordship, the same wheel the twelve signs traverse. Body gate: knees — what you bow to in order to participate.

VII. Closing — whose vibration governs you?

Past generations enslaved themselves and their descendants by words of reception — slogans of identity, elemental allegiance, rulers named at the gates of the body.

A people electing rulers today repeat that pattern when they say, in effect: You will govern our head, our wealth, our speech, our wombs, our knees, our times — and we receive your platform as our destiny.

Ephesians 6:12 names the powers behind such arrangements — not flesh and blood alone, but ἀρχαί, ἐξουσίαι, κοσμοκράτορες τοῦ σκότους τοῦ αἰῶνος τούτου.

The twelve prayers renounce the slogans and receive the gates back under the only lawful King.

Stand therefore (Eph 6:13–18): truth, righteousness, gospel of peace, faith, salvation, Spirit, prayer — and do not speak allegiance to the wrong lords.
