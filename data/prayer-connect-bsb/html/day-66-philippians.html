Genesis to Revelation · Day 66 of 82

Philippians

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Philippians 2:9-11

A name which is above every name; every knee should bow.

2:9Therefore God exalted Him to the highest place and gave Him the name above all names,

2:10that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth,

2:11and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

Philippians 3:13-14

Forgetting those things which are behind; press toward the mark.

3:13Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead,

3:14I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.
