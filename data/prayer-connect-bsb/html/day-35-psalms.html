Genesis to Revelation · Day 35 of 82

Psalms

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Psalms 7:8-9, 16

Judge me, O LORD; mischief return upon his own head.

7:8The LORD judges the peoples; vindicate me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and integrity.

7:9Put an end to the evil of the wicked, but establish the righteous, O righteous God who searches hearts and minds.

7:16His trouble recoils on himself, and his violence falls on his own head.

Psalms 32:1-5

Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven; I acknowledged.

32:1Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered.

32:2Blessed is the man whose iniquity the LORD does not count against him, in whose spirit there is no deceit.

32:3When I kept silent, my bones became brittle from my groaning all day long.

32:4For day and night Your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was drained as in the summer heat.Selah

32:5Then I acknowledged my sin to You and did not hide my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and You forgave the guilt of my sin.Selah

Psalms 51:1-17

Against Thee; wash me; a broken spirit; then sacrifice.

51:1Have mercy on me, O God, according to Your loving devotion; according to Your great compassion, blot out my transgressions.

51:2Wash me clean of my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.

51:3For I know my transgressions, and my sin is always before me.

51:4Against You, You only, have I sinned and done what is evil in Your sight, so that You may be proved right when You speak and blameless when You judge.

51:5Surely I was brought forth in iniquity; I was sinful when my mother conceived me.

51:6Surely You desire truth in the inmost being; You teach me wisdom in the inmost place.

51:7Purify me with hyssop, and I will be clean; wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

51:8Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones You have crushed rejoice.

51:9Hide Your face from my sins and blot out all my iniquities.

51:10Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.

51:11Cast me not away from Your presence; take not Your Holy Spirit from me.

51:12Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and sustain me with a willing spirit.

51:13Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners will return to You.

51:14Deliver me from bloodguilt, O God, the God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing of Your righteousness.

51:15O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare Your praise.

51:16For You do not delight in sacrifice, or I would bring it; You take no pleasure in burnt offerings.

51:17The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and a contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.

Psalms 78:1-8

We will not hide from their children; a generation that set not their heart aright.

78:1Give ear, O my people, to my instruction; listen to the words of my mouth.

78:2I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from the beginning,

78:3that we have heard and known and our fathers have relayed to us.

78:4We will not hide them from their children but will declare to the next generation the praises of the LORD and His might and the wonders He has performed.

78:5For He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to teach to their children,

78:6that the coming generation would know them— even children yet to be born— to arise and tell their own children

78:7that they should put their confidence in God, not forgetting His works, but keeping His commandments.

78:8Then they will not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, whose heart was not loyal, whose spirit was not faithful to God.

Psalms 79:8-9

Remember not against us former iniquities.

79:8Do not hold past sins against us; let Your compassion come quickly, for we are brought low.

79:9Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of Your name; deliver us and atone for our sins, for the sake of Your name.

Psalms 85:1-7

Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of Thy people; turn us.

85:1You showed favor to Your land, O LORD; You restored Jacob from captivity.

85:2You forgave the iniquity of Your people; You covered all their sin.Selah

85:3You withheld all Your fury; You turned from Your burning anger.

85:4Restore us, O God of our salvation, and put away Your displeasure toward us.

85:5Will You be angry with us forever? Will You draw out Your anger to all generations?

85:6Will You not revive us again, that Your people may rejoice in You?

85:7Show us Your loving devotion, O LORD, and grant us Your salvation.

Psalms 90:8

Thou hast set our iniquities before Thee.

90:8You have set our iniquities before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence.

Psalms 103:17-18

Mercy from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear Him, and His righteousness unto children's children.

103:17But from everlasting to everlasting the loving devotion of the LORD extends to those who fear Him, and His righteousness to their children’s children—

103:18to those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His precepts.

Psalms 106:6-7

We have sinned with our fathers.

106:6We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly.

106:7Our fathers in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders or remember Your abundant kindness; but they rebelled by the sea, there at the Red Sea.

Psalms 109:14-15

Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered.

109:14May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and the sin of his mother never be blotted out.

109:15May their sins always remain before the LORD, that He may cut off their memory from the earth.

Psalms 127:3-5

Children are an heritage of the LORD.

127:3Children are indeed a heritage from the LORD, and the fruit of the womb is His reward.

127:4Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are children born in one’s youth.

127:5Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them. He will not be put to shame when he confronts the enemies at the gate.

Psalms 139:23-24

Search me; know my thoughts; lead me.

139:23Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns.

139:24See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting.
