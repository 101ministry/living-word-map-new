Proverbs

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Proverbs 3:5-7

Trust in the LORD; be not wise in thine own eyes.

3:5Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding;

3:6in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight.

3:7Be not wise in your own eyes; fear the LORD and turn away from evil.

Proverbs 4:23

Keep thy heart with all diligence.

4:23Guard your heart with all diligence, for from it flow springs of life.

Proverbs 11:21

The seed of the righteous shall be delivered.

11:21Be assured that the wicked will not go unpunished, but the offspring of the righteous will escape.

Proverbs 13:22

A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children.

13:22A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children, but the sinner’s wealth is passed to the righteous.

Proverbs 14:26

In the fear of the LORD is strong confidence; His children shall have a place of refuge.

14:26He who fears the LORD is secure in confidence, and his children shall have a place of refuge.

Proverbs 16:31

A crown of glory if found in the way of righteousness.

16:31Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is attained along the path of righteousness.

Proverbs 20:7

The just man's children are blessed after him.

20:7The righteous man walks with integrity; blessed are his children after him.

Proverbs 22:6

Train up a child in the way he should go.

22:6Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.

Proverbs 28:13

He that covereth his sins shall not prosper.

28:13He who conceals his sins will not prosper, but whoever confesses and renounces them will find mercy.
