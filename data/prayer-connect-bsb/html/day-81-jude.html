Genesis to Revelation · Day 81 of 82

Jude

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Jude 3-4

Contend for the faith; ungodly men crept in unawares.

1:3Beloved, although I made every effort to write to you about the salvation we share, I felt it necessary to write and urge you to contend earnestly for the faith entrusted once for all to the saints.

1:4For certain men have crept in among you unnoticed—ungodly ones who were designated long ago for condemnation. They turn the grace of our God into a license for immorality, and they deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

Jude 20-25

Building up yourselves; keep yourselves in the love of God; unto Him that is able to keep you from falling.

1:20But you, beloved, by building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit,

1:21keep yourselves in the love of God as you await the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ to bring you eternal life.

1:22And indeed, have mercy on those who doubt;

1:23save others by snatching them from the fire; and to still others show mercy tempered with fear, hating even the clothing stained by the flesh.

1:24Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you unblemished in His glorious presence, with great joy—

1:25to the only God our Savior be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord before all time, and now, and for all eternity. Amen.
