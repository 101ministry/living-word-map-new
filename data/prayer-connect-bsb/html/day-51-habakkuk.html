Genesis to Revelation · Day 51 of 82

Habakkuk

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Habakkuk 1:13

Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil.

1:13Your eyes are too pure to look upon evil, and You cannot tolerate wrongdoing. So why do You tolerate the faithless? Why are You silent while the wicked swallow up those more righteous than themselves?

Habakkuk 2:4

The just shall live by his faith.

2:4Look at the proud one; his soul is not upright — but the righteous will live by faith —

Habakkuk 3:2

O LORD, revive Thy work; in wrath remember mercy.

3:2O LORD, I have heard the report of You; I stand in awe, O LORD, of Your deeds. Revive them in these years; make them known in these years. In Your wrath, remember mercy!
