Genesis to Revelation · Day 64 of 82

Galatians

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Galatians 3:13-14, 16, 29

Christ hath redeemed us from the curse; the blessing of Abraham on the Gentiles; to thy seed, which is Christ; if ye be Christ's, then Abraham's seed.

3:13Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us. For it is written: “Cursed is everyone who is hung on a tree.”

3:14He redeemed us in order that the blessing promised to Abraham would come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, so that by faith we might receive the promise of the Spirit.

3:16The promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. The Scripture does not say, “and to seeds,” meaning many, but “and to your seed,” meaning One, who is Christ.

3:29And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s seed and heirs according to the promise.

Galatians 4:3-9

In bondage under the elements; how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly?

4:3So also, when we were children, we were enslaved under the basic principles of the world.

4:4But when the time had fully come, God sent His Son, born of a woman, born under the law,

4:5to redeem those under the law, that we might receive our adoption as sons.

4:6And because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying out, “Abba, Father!”

4:7So you are no longer a slave, but a son; and since you are a son, you are also an heir through God.

4:8Formerly, when you did not know God, you were slaves to those who by nature are not gods.

4:9But now that you know God, or rather are known by God, how is it that you are turning back to those weak and worthless principles? Do you wish to be enslaved by them all over again?

Galatians 5:1, 19-21

Stand fast in liberty; works of the flesh.

5:1It is for freedom that Christ has set us free. Stand firm, then, and do not be encumbered once more by a yoke of slavery.

5:19The acts of the flesh are obvious: sexual immorality, impurity, and debauchery;

5:20idolatry and sorcery; hatred, discord, jealousy, and rage; rivalries, divisions, factions,

5:21and envy; drunkenness, orgies, and the like. I warn you, as I did before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

Galatians 6:1-8

Restore; bear burdens; whatsoever a man soweth.

6:1Brothers, if someone is caught in a trespass, you who are spiritual should restore him with a spirit of gentleness. But watch yourself, or you also may be tempted.

6:2Carry one another’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.

6:3If anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.

6:4Each one should test his own work. Then he will have reason to boast in himself alone, and not in someone else.

6:5For each one should carry his own load.

6:6Nevertheless, the one who receives instruction in the word must share in all good things with his instructor.

6:7Do not be deceived: God is not to be mocked. Whatever a man sows, he will reap in return.

6:8The one who sows to please his flesh, from the flesh will reap destruction; but the one who sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit will reap eternal life.
