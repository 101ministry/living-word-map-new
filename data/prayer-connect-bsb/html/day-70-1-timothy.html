Genesis to Revelation · Day 70 of 82

1 Timothy

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

1 Timothy 1:18-20

War a good warfare; holding faith and a good conscience.

1:18Timothy, my child, I entrust you with this command in keeping with the previous prophecies about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,

1:19holding on to faith and a good conscience, which some have rejected and thereby shipwrecked their faith.

1:20Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan to be taught not to blaspheme.

1 Timothy 2:1-6

Prayers for all men; one Mediator; who gave Himself a ransom.

2:1First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgiving be offered for everyone—

2:2for kings and all those in authority—so that we may lead tranquil and quiet lives in all godliness and dignity.

2:3This is good and pleasing in the sight of God our Savior,

2:4who wants everyone to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.

2:5For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,

2:6who gave Himself as a ransom for all—the testimony that was given at just the right time.

1 Timothy 4:1-2

Seducing spirits; doctrines of devils; conscience seared.

4:1Now the Spirit expressly states that in later times some will abandon the faith to follow deceitful spirits and the teachings of demons,

4:2influenced by the hypocrisy of liars, whose consciences are seared with a hot iron.

1 Timothy 5:8, 22

Provide for his own house; neither be partaker of other men's sins.

5:8If anyone does not provide for his own, and especially his own household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

5:22Do not be too quick in the laying on of hands and thereby share in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.
