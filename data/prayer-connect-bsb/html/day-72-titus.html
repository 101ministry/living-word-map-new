Genesis to Revelation · Day 72 of 82

Titus

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Titus 1:15-16

Unto the pure all things are pure; they deny Him in works.

1:15To the pure, all things are pure; but to the defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure. Indeed, both their minds and their consciences are defiled.

1:16They profess to know God, but by their actions they deny Him. They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit for any good deed.

Titus 2:11-14

The grace of God; redeem us from all iniquity.

2:11For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to everyone.

2:12It instructs us to renounce ungodliness and worldly passions, and to live sensible, upright, and godly lives in the present age,

2:13as we await the blessed hope and glorious appearance of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ.

2:14He gave Himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for Himself a people for His own possession, zealous for good deeds.

Titus 3:3-7

We ourselves were sometimes foolish; according to His mercy He saved us.

3:3For at one time we too were foolish, disobedient, misled, and enslaved to all sorts of desires and pleasures—living in malice and envy, being hated and hating one another.

3:4But when the kindness of God our Savior and His love for mankind appeared,

3:5He saved us, not by the righteous deeds we had done, but according to His mercy, through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit.

3:6This is the Spirit He poured out on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Savior,

3:7so that, having been justified by His grace, we would become heirs with the hope of eternal life.
