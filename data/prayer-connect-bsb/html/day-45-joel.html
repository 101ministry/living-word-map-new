Genesis to Revelation · Day 45 of 82

Joel

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Joel 1:3

Tell ye your children, and let your children tell their children.

1:3Tell it to your children; let your children tell it to their children, and their children to the next generation.

Joel 2:12-18

Turn ye even to Me with all your heart; the LORD will be jealous for His land.

2:12“Yet even now,” declares the LORD, “return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning.”

2:13So rend your hearts and not your garments, and return to the LORD your God. For He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion. And He relents from sending disaster.

2:14Who knows? He may turn and relent and leave a blessing behind Him— grain and drink offerings for the LORD your God.

2:15Blow the ram’s horn in Zion, consecrate a fast, proclaim a sacred assembly.

2:16Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the aged, gather the children, even those nursing at the breast. Let the bridegroom leave his room, and the bride her chamber.

2:17Let the priests who minister before the LORD weep between the portico and the altar, saying, “Spare Your people, O LORD, and do not make Your heritage a reproach, an object of scorn among the nations. Why should they say among the peoples, ‘Where is their God?’”

2:18Then the LORD became jealous for His land, and He spared His people.

Joel 2:28-29

I will pour out My spirit upon all flesh; your sons and your daughters.

2:28And afterward, I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your old men will dream dreams, your young men will see visions.

2:29Even on My menservants and maidservants, I will pour out My Spirit in those days.
