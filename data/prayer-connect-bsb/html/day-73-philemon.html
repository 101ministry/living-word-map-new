Genesis to Revelation · Day 73 of 82

Philemon

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Philemon 10-18

Receive him as myself; if he hath wronged thee, put that on mine account — representative covering in a household.

1:10I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whose father I became while I was in chains.

1:11Formerly he was useless to you, but now he has become useful both to you and to me.

1:12I am sending back to you him who is my very heart.

1:13I would have liked to keep him with me, so that on your behalf he could minister to me in my chains for the gospel.

1:14But I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your goodness will not be out of compulsion, but by your own free will.

1:15For perhaps this is why he was separated from you for a while, so that you might have him back for good—

1:16no longer as a slave, but better than a slave, as a beloved brother. He is especially beloved to me, but even more so to you, both in person and in the Lord.

1:17So if you consider me a partner, receive him as you would receive me.

1:18But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account.
