Genesis to Revelation · Day 67 of 82

Colossians

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

Colossians 1:13-14

Delivered from the power of darkness; redemption through His blood.

1:13He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son,

1:14in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins.

Colossians 2:13-15

Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances; spoiled principalities.

2:13When you were dead in your trespasses and in the uncircumcision of your sinful nature, God made you alive with Christ. He forgave us all our trespasses,

2:14having canceled the debt ascribed to us in the decrees that stood against us. He took it away, nailing it to the cross!

2:15And having disarmed the powers and authorities, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them by the cross.

Colossians 3:2-14

Set your affection above; put off anger; forgive as Christ forgave.

3:2Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.

3:3For you died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God.

3:4When Christ, who is your life, appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory.

3:5Put to death, therefore, the components of your earthly nature: sexual immorality, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed, which is idolatry.

3:6Because of these, the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience.

3:7When you lived among them, you also used to walk in these ways.

3:8But now you must put aside all such things as these: anger, rage, malice, slander, and filthy language from your lips.

3:9Do not lie to one another, since you have taken off the old self with its practices,

3:10and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge in the image of its Creator.

3:11Here there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, or free, but Christ is all and is in all.

3:12Therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, clothe yourselves with hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.

3:13Bear with one another and forgive any complaint you may have against someone else. Forgive as the Lord forgave you.

3:14And over all these virtues put on love, which is the bond of perfect unity.
