Genesis to Revelation · Day 78 of 82

1 John

Passages that underwrite bloodline repentance as the prayers practice it.

1 John 1:7-9

The blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanseth us from all sin; if we confess.

1:7But if we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus His Son cleanses us from all sin.

1:8If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.

1:9If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

1 John 2:1-2

We have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.

2:1My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate before the Father—Jesus Christ, the Righteous One.

2:2He Himself is the atoning sacrifice for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the sins of the whole world.

1 John 3:8

For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that He might destroy the works of the devil.

3:8The one who practices sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the very start. This is why the Son of God was revealed, to destroy the works of the devil.

1 John 4:4

Greater is He that is in you, than he that is in the world.

4:4You, little children, are from God and have overcome them, because greater is He who is in you than he who is in the world.

1 John 5:16-21

Sin not unto death; keep yourselves from idols.

5:16If anyone sees his brother committing a sin not leading to death, he should ask God, who will give life to those who commit this kind of sin. There is a sin that leads to death; I am not saying he should ask regarding that sin.

5:17All unrighteousness is sin, yet there is sin that does not lead to death.

5:18We know that anyone born of God does not keep on sinning; the One who was born of God protects him, and the evil one cannot touch him.

5:19We know that we are of God, and that the whole world is under the power of the evil one.

5:20And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know Him who is true; and we are in Him who is true—in His Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life.

5:21Little children, keep yourselves from idols.
