How the prayers connect · Day 14 of 82

I cast off of my head the crown of iniquity that had been attached to my head. You are banned from my life, my mind, my heart, and my body.

All rounds

Iniquity sat on the head as a claim of rule. Aaron's plate bore iniquity on the forehead until atonement. The prayers refuse that crown and shut the gates of life, mind, heart, and body.

Ex 28:36-38

Holy to the LORD on the forehead; Aaron bears the iniquity of the holy things.

28:36You are to make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it as on a seal: HOLY TO THE LORD.

28:37Fasten to it a blue cord to mount it on the turban; it shall be on the front of the turban.

28:38And it will be worn on Aaron’s forehead, so that he may bear the iniquity of the holy things that the sons of Israel consecrate with regard to all their holy gifts. It shall always be on his forehead, so that they may be acceptable before the LORD.
