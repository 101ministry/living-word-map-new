How the prayers connect · Day 11 of 82

I ask You Father to forgive my bloodline since I have forgiven other bloodlines. I ask You to judge between me and them so that the judgment is pure and holy.

All rounds (Round 1: forgive me since I have forgiven others)

Remission on earth is tied to remission received. Then the Father, not the intercessor, judges between lines so the case is clean.

Gen 16:5

The LORD judge between me and thee.

16:5Then Sarai said to Abram, “May the wrong done to me be upon you! I delivered my servant into your arms, and ever since she saw that she was pregnant, she has treated me with contempt. May the LORD judge between you and me.”

Gen 31:53

The God of Abraham judge between us.

31:53May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, the God of their father, judge between us.” So Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.

1 Sam 24:12, 15

The LORD judge between me and thee.

24:12May the LORD judge between you and me, and may the LORD take vengeance on you, but my hand will never be against you.

24:15May the LORD be our judge and decide between you and me. May He take notice and plead my case and deliver me from your hand.”

1 Sam 26:23

The LORD render to every man his righteousness.

26:23May the LORD repay every man for his righteousness and faithfulness. For the LORD delivered you into my hand today, but I would not extend my hand against the LORD’s anointed.

2 Chr 19:6-7

You judge not for man, but for the LORD; no iniquity with the LORD.

19:6Then he said to the judges, “Consider carefully what you do, for you are not judging for man, but for the LORD, who is with you when you render judgment.

19:7And now, may the fear of the LORD be upon you. Be careful what you do, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice or partiality or bribery.”

Ps 7:8-9

Judge me, O LORD; let wickedness of the wicked come to an end.

7:8The LORD judges the peoples; vindicate me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and integrity.

7:9Put an end to the evil of the wicked, but establish the righteous, O righteous God who searches hearts and minds.

Ps 82:1-8

God stands in the congregation of the mighty; judge righteously.

82:6I have said, ‘You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High.’
