Understanding Life in Simplified Terms

Plain-language explanations for every word on the Amojo Life emotion and attitude lists

Sources: Amojo Life resource lists (positive/negative emotions and attitudes). Words marked * appear on a list where they usually belong on the opposite side.

Positive Emotions

Source: https://amojolife.wordpress.com/resources/positive-emotions-list/ - 76 words

Absorbed
So into something (a game, book, project) that the rest of the world fades out for a while.
Accepting
Okay with how things are — people, situations, yourself — without fighting reality every second.
Accommodating
Willing to adjust and make room for others so things can work for everyone.
Accomplished
That "I actually did it" feeling after finishing something that took real effort.
Adaptable
You can roll with change — new school, new plan, surprise problem — without totally falling apart.
Adversarial
Usually negative: treating people like opponents you have to beat. Odd on this list.
Agreeable
Easy to get along with; you are willing to cooperate instead of picking fights.
Alert
Switched on — noticing what is happening around you and ready to respond.
Altruistic
You care about helping others even when there is nothing in it for you.
Analytical
You break problems into pieces and think them through logically.
Approving
You look at someone or something and think, "Yeah, that is good / that works."
Authentic
Real — you are not faking a personality to impress people.
Balanced
Not living in extremes; work, rest, emotions, and relationships feel steadier.
Beautiful
Seeing or feeling beauty — in art, nature, a moment, or even yourself.
Brave
You do the right or hard thing even when you are scared.
Calm
Settled inside — not revved up, not panicking; your nervous system is chill.
Cheerful
Upbeat mood; you bring a little light into the room.
Compassionate
You feel with people who are hurting and want to help, not just stare.
Confident
You trust you can handle stuff — or at least try without collapsing.
Content
Not chasing more every second; you are okay with what you have right now.
Cooperative
You work with people instead of against them.
Courageous
Same family as brave — stepping up when it costs you something.
Creative
You make new ideas, art, solutions, or jokes that did not exist before.
Curious
You actually want to know how things work and why people do what they do.
Dignified
You carry yourself with self-respect — not sloppy, not desperate for approval.
Eccentric
A little weird in your own unique way — and you are fine with that.
Ecstatic
Over-the-top happy — like winning something huge or hearing the best news ever.
Empathic
You can feel what someone else might be feeling, not just your own side.
Empowered
You believe you have real say in your life and can make things happen.
Excited
Amped up in a good way — something good is coming or happening.
Expressive
You let your feelings and thoughts show — through words, art, humor, whatever.
Extroverted
You get energy from people; being around others charges you up.
Fair
You try to be even-handed — not playing favorites or bending rules for yourself.
Faithful
Loyal and steady — you stick to people, promises, or beliefs you have committed to.
Glad
Happy about something specific — "I am glad you came," "glad that is over."
Grateful
You notice what is good and actually appreciate it instead of taking it for granted.
Happy
General good mood — life feels lighter and more pleasant.
Helpful
You jump in to make someone's load easier when you can.
Hesitant
Usually negative: holding back because you are unsure or scared. Odd on this list.
Idealistic
You believe things should be better and you care about big values like justice or truth.
Individualistic
You think for yourself and do not just copy the crowd to fit in.
Inspired
Something sparked you — an idea, person, song — and now you want to do something.
Interested
Something catches your attention and you want to learn or engage more.
Introspective
You look inward — thinking about your motives, patterns, and what you really feel.
Invulnerable
Feeling like nothing can touch you — strong, protected, untouchable. (Can be real confidence or denial.)
Joyful
Deep, bright happiness — not just "fine," but genuinely glad to be alive.
Likable
People enjoy being around you; you are pleasant without being fake.
Lively
Full of energy and spark; you animate a room.
Loved
You feel cared for and valued by someone — not alone in that way.
Loving
You are giving care, warmth, and loyalty to others.
Mediating
You help two sides calm down and find middle ground instead of escalating.
Open
Willing to hear new ideas, feelings, and experiences without slamming the door.
Passionate
You care hard about something — a cause, person, hobby, goal.
Peaceful
Inner quiet — little internal war, even if the outside world is noisy.
Pleased
Satisfied with how something turned out — a grade, a conversation, a meal.
Powerful
You feel capable and influential — like your choices actually matter.
Practical
You focus on what actually works in real life, not just cool theories.
Proud
Healthy pride: you are glad about something you did or who you are becoming.
Purposeful
Your life feels like it is going somewhere that means something.
Rejoicing
Celebrating hard — gratitude and happiness spilling out.
Responsible
You own your duties and follow through on what you said you would do.
Self accepting
You are okay being you — flaws included — without constant self-attack.
Sensitive
You pick up on vibes and feelings (yours and others') — can be a strength.
Serene
Deep calm — like a still lake inside you.
Serenity
The state of that calm — quiet steadiness.
Stable
Emotions and life do not feel like a roller coaster every hour.
Stimulated
Your brain is engaged and awake — interested, challenged, alive.
Tolerant
You can live alongside differences — opinions, styles, backgrounds — without freaking out.
Understanding
You get where someone is coming from, even if you disagree.
Unconcerned
Not worried or bothered — either healthy chill or not caring when you should. (Depends.)
Visionary
You see possibilities others miss — future ideas, big dreams, new paths.
Well-meaning
Your intentions are good even if you sometimes mess up the execution.
Wise
You have learned from life and give advice or choices that actually hold up over time.
Worthy
You feel you deserve respect, love, and a place in the world — not garbage.
In service
You are oriented toward helping others, not just yourself.
Important
You feel your life and role matter — you are not invisible.

Considerate

Cooperative
Team-player mindset — shared win over solo glory.
Courageous
Willing to face fear, speak up, or do hard things when it counts.
Decisive
You can pick a direction and commit instead of spinning forever.
Determined
Once you decide, you keep pushing even when it gets ugly.
Devoted
Loyal and all-in toward someone or something you have pledged yourself to.
Embraces
You welcome experiences, people, or truth instead of dodging them.
Endurance
You can keep going through boredom, pain, or delay without quitting immediately.
Enthusiastic
Genuinely pumped — your energy shows you care.
Expansive
Big-hearted and open — room for ideas, people, and possibilities.
Faith
Trust in God, people, process, or hope — believing good can hold even when you cannot see it yet.
Flexible
You bend when plans change instead of snapping.
Focused
You lock in on what matters and do not drift every five seconds.
Forgiving
You release grudges and let people (including yourself) move forward after hurt.
Freedom
Valuing the ability to choose, grow, and not be controlled — for you and others.
Friendly
Approachable and kind toward people; you do not act like everyone is an enemy.
Frugal
Smart with money and time — not wasteful, not reckless.
Generous
You give — time, money, attention, credit — without hoarding everything.
Goodwill
You want good for others, not harm; basic "I am on your side" energy.
Grateful
Habit of noticing and thanking for what is good in life.
Hard working
You show up and put in effort even when motivation dips.
Honest
Truth is your default — you do not build your life on lies.
Humble
You know you are not the center of the universe; you can learn and apologize.
Involved
You participate — you do not sit on the sidelines of your own life or community.
Kind
You treat people with gentleness and respect, especially when you could be harsh.
Motivated
You have inner drive pushing you toward goals.
Not jealous
You can celebrate others' wins without secretly wanting to tear them down.
Open minded
Willing to consider ideas that are not yours yet.
Optimistic
You expect things can work out — not blind, but hopeful.
Perseverance
Keep-going grit when everything says stop.
Positive
You look for solutions and good possibilities instead of drowning in doom.
Practical
Feet-on-the-ground thinking — what will actually work Monday morning?
Punctual
You respect time — yours and others' — by showing up when you said you would.
Realistic
You see things as they are, not only as you wish they were.
Reliable
People can count on you to do what you said.
Responsible
You own your role, mistakes, and duties.
Responsive
When someone reaches out, you answer — you do not ghost the people who matter.
Self-confident
You trust your ability to learn, decide, and handle life.
Self-directed
You steer your choices — not just parents, trends, or fear.
Self-disciplined
You can delay gratification and stick to rules you chose.
Self-esteem
Healthy sense that you have value even when you fail a test or lose a friend.
Self-giving
You invest yourself for others' good, not only your comfort.
Selfless
You consider others' needs alongside (or before) your own when it matters.
Self-reliant
You can stand on your own feet and solve problems without always needing a rescue.
Sensitive
Attuned to feelings and atmosphere — you notice what others miss.
Serious
You take important things seriously instead of joking through everything.
Sincere
What you say matches what you mean — no performance personality.
Social independence
You are okay being yourself in groups; you do not need everyone's approval to function.
Sympathetic
You feel for people who are struggling and show it.
Thoughtful
You think before you speak or act; you consider impact.
Thoughtful towards others
Specifically aimed at not being careless with people's hearts.
Trusting
You give people reasonable benefit of the doubt instead of assuming the worst.
Tolerant
Live-and-let-live toward differences that do not require war.
Unpretentiousness
No fake "I am better than you" act — down-to-earth.
Unselfish
You do not hog everything — spotlight, resources, last slice — for yourself.
Willingness
Ready to try, help, change, or show up when asked.
Work-oriented
You value effort and getting things done over pure vibes.
Interested
Engaged with learning and people — curiosity as a lifestyle.
Distant
Usually negative: keeping emotional space on purpose. Odd on this list.
Gloomy
Negative: dark, pessimistic mood. Definitely misplaced here.
Responsibility
The attitude that "this is mine to carry" — ownership of your part in life.
