You go. Galatians 6, 9 says, And let us not lose heart and grow weary and faint in acting nobly and doing right. For in due time and at the appointed season we shall reap. If we do not loosen and relax our courage and faint, the enemy wants to make you discouraged so that he can steal your promises and stop your progress. Being courageous is also linked to reaping in your appointed season. When you allow your heart to become discouraged and grow weary, then your courage is in danger. Jesus said to his disciples, I have told you these things so that in me you may have peace and confidence. In the world you will have tribulations and trials and distress and frustrations, but be of good cheer. In the Amplified Version it says, Take courage, be confident, certain, undaunted, for I have overcome the world. I have deprived it of its power to harm you and have conquered it for you. So courage is not a feeling. You don't wake up and say, I feel courageous today. Courage is something that must be taken on. It must be grabbed hold of. It must be stood in. It does not rely on anything on the outside, but solely on the promises of God and God that is inside of you. 1 John 4 verse 4 reads, He are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because greater is he that is in you than he that is in the world. Your faith in Christ will cause you to be fearless. It will bring about a confidence, even though your situation doesn't look good. With your developed courage, you will walk through tribulations, distresses, situations, and whatever else life can throw at you. But the choice is ultimately yours. Do you move in discouragement, or do you walk confidently, boldly, in courage? God bless you.
