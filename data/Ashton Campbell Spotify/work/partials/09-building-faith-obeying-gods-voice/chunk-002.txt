The walk was completed. He saved his family and many animals and the earth started again when the waters had settled. Each of these men chose to be obedient to God and not their flesh. How then can you receive the anointing for the assignment if you are in an ungovernable state? And how will you see the full vision of God if your vision is only on yourself and not the plight of others? Nehemiah fasted and prayed constantly. He prayed and he fasted and he asked God for answers. Deuteronomy chapter 28 verses 1 and 2 reads, Now it shall come to pass, if you diligently obey the voice of the Lord your God, to observe carefully all his commandments, which I commanded you today, that the Lord your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth, and all the blessings shall come upon you and overtake you, because you obey the voice of the Lord your God. It comes as a big surprise to those who start their repentance journey, and they do a repentance that allows them to move away from self and start serving for the God. How quickly things start to progress within their lives in a manner that they had not expected. The word of God says that the blessing will come on you and overtake you, and God listed all of those blessings. Your bonds will be full. You will build more bonds. You will be blessed when you go in. You will be blessed when you leave. Whatever you set your hand to do will be successful. You will be the head and not the tail. Repentance and obedience go hand in hand for the blessings to come upon your life. But you cannot do it without faith. You've got to believe God. Trust God. Release your own way of thinking and living and live for God, so that God can be made strong in your weakness. God bless you.
