That power God has given us. Psalm 56 verse 11 reads, In God I have put my trust, and I will not be afraid what man can do unto me. Just like the phone and the cable, if we try to do anything in our own strength, eventually we will run out of power. But if we stay connected to the source, we will have an unlimited amount of power. At our disposal for the task and the situations that are before us. It is your job to rely on God solely for this power. As Ephesians 6 verse 10 reads, Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might. In our covenant relationship with God, God is the stronger partner. It is because of His strength that we are able to face all situations and all enemies that are coming head on in our lives. We should never lean on our own understanding and our own strength. The word of God says, not by strength, not by might, but by my spirit. God bless you.
