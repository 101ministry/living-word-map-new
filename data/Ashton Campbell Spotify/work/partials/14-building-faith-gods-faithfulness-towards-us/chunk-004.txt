Father God is changing the spiritual space and dimensions in the heaven above you and as well as on earth. This is in fact a time to meet God intimately in prayer as an act of worship, to hear what he has to say, to study the word so that you will know God's character. Act on every action that God and the Holy Spirit reveals, irrespective of the physical domain surrounding you. Do not let your eyes deter you. Enter into warfare and claim back your inheritance, like Isaac when he went to take the stones of circumstance out of his well of blessings. As you grow deeper into an understanding and relationship of intimacy with Abba Father, you will put your faith in his faithfulness and never be disappointed. Then you can be like Deuteronomy chapter 7 verse 9 that says, Know, recognize and understand therefore that the Lord, your God, he is God, the faithful God. He keeps covenant and steadfast love and mercy with those who love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations. And 1 Thessalonians chapter 5 from verse 23 to 24 says, And may the God of peace himself sanctify you through and through, that is separate from your profane things, make you pure and holy, consecrated to God. And may your spirit and soul and body be preserved, sound and complete and found blameless at the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ, the Messiah. Faithful is he who is calling you to himself and utterly trustworthy, and he will do it, fulfill his call by hollering and keeping you. You see, God is faithful, and that is why it is for us to have faith in his faithfulness towards us. Let us sit back and contemplate on the goodness of God, and not allow the circumstances around us to deter us from believing that our Father is for us, building us up to be heirs of the kingdom of heaven. God bless you all.
