Gates Prayer

Heavenly Father, I come to you today. I recognize that my Ears, Eyes, Nose, Mouth, and Hands are full of iniquity. Instead of using my measurement of numbers, I will use your measurement of just one making the slate black.

I bring to You my Eye Gate, the physical one. I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and memories of what I have seen that You would not see. In my soul, I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and muscle memories of what I was pretty sure I saw in others. Between my soul gate and spirit gate, I ask for the Cross of Christ to stand between. Remind me to ask: Is this the type of thing Jesus would have seen on the Cross?

I bring to You my Ear Gate, the physical one. I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and memories of what I have heard that You would not hear. In my soul, I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and muscle memories of what I was pretty sure I heard in others. Between my soul gate and spirit gate, I ask for the Cross of Christ to stand between. Remind me to ask: Is this the type of thing Jesus would have listened to on the Cross?

I bring to you my Nose Gate, the physical one. I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and memories of what I have smelled or felt that You would not feel. In my soul, I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and muscle memories of what I was pretty sure I felt from others. Between my soul and spirit, I ask for the Cross of Christ to stand between. Remind me to ask: Is this the type of thing Jesus would have felt from others on the Cross?

I bring to you my Mouth Gate, the physical one. I ask you to burn out all ungodly projections and memories of what I have said that You would not say. In my soul, I ask you to burn out all ungodly projections and muscle memories of what I was pretty sure I was supposed to say about or to others. Between my soul and spirit, I ask for the Cross of Christ to stand between. Remind me to ask: Is this the type of thing Jesus would have said on the Cross?

I bring to you my Touch Gate, the physical one. I ask you to burn out all ungodly perceptions and memories of what I have created in my life with my actions, words, and thoughts that You would not act on, speak on, or think. In my soul, I ask you to burn out all ungodly creations and muscle memories of what I was pretty sure I had to create my worldview with ungodly thoughts, ungodly words, and ungodly actions. Between my soul and spirit, I ask for the Cross of Christ to stand between. Remind me to ask: Is this the type of thing Jesus would have thought about, spoke about, or acted on while on the Cross?

Finally, between my Spiritual Eyes, Ears, Nose, Mouth, and Touch senses, I ask you to burn out all perceptions, projections, and soul ties that exist between me and every person and every object I have had any type of interaction with. My soul battles enough without having anchors to how I perceive others or what I project about them. Burn out the chaff, the things I do not need. Your Word says that out of the heart and soul come the things that defile. Lord, as Master of my life, burn all the defilement out of my soul and burn all soul ties today. In the mighty name of Jesus, Amen.
