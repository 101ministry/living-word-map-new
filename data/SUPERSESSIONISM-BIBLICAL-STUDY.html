ESV in paragraph context | Blue Letter Bible: MacArthur, Chuck Smith C2000, Matthew Henry (selective) | Landscape layout

Short answer: The list of passages arguing that Gentiles are added rather than replacing ethnic Israel is significantly longer and more explicit than the list commonly cited for hard supersessionism (~19 primary replacement passages vs ~45+ covenant / grafting passages).

Three positions: (1) Hard supersessionism - Church replaces Israel; promises to ethnic Israel belong only to the Church. (2) Soft supersessionism / fulfillment - Church fulfills Israel's mission in Christ; possible future for ethnic Israel. (3) Restoration / dual-track - Israel and Church have distinct but related roles.

Important: No replacement passage explicitly says "the Church has replaced Israel." Advocates infer from judgment, fulfillment, new covenant, or Gentile inclusion. Romans 11 explicitly warns Gentiles not to boast over Israel and calls God's gifts and calling irrevocable.
