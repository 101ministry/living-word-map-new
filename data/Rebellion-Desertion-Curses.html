Summary

Named disease/affliction terms (distinct Hebrew roots)

  • 17 distinct terms if ophalim (H6076) and techorim (H2914) are counted separately.
  • 16 distinct terms if those two are treated as one hemorrhoid/tumor complex (as many English Bibles render them).
  • Physical/bodily: pestilence, consumption, fever, inflammation, burning heat, boils (Egypt), tumors, hemorrhoids, scab, itch, severe boils (full body), failing eyes.
  • Mental/sensory: madness, blindness, confusion of heart, maddened (v. 34), trembling heart, despair of soul.
  • Collective formulas: great plagues and sicknesses (v. 59); all diseases of Egypt (v. 60); every sickness and plague not in this book (v. 61).

Rebellion + Desertion = Curses — Deuteronomy 28 opens the curse section (v. 15) with disobedience to the LORD’s voice and commandments. The diseases listed above are covenant sanctions for that rebellion and forsaking of God (cf. v. 20).
