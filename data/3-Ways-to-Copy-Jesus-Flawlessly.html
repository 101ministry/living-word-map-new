3 Ways to Copy Jesus Flawlessly

The acceptable, good, and perfect will of God — three requisites, three levels

There is a reality called the perfect will of God. Another called the good will of God. Finally the acceptable will of God.

Within the perfect will, 3 of 3 requisites have been completed. Within the good will, 2 of 3. Within the acceptable will, 1 of 3.

The three requisites are themselves three-fold: seek, knock, and ask; lay your life down, pick up the cross, and follow / replicate Jesus; and pray one hour, two hours, or three hours in Holy Spirit tongues. To walk in a given will, complete that many steps of each of the three.

Romans 12:1–2 — Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

The map at a glance

Will of God Measure Way 1 — Seek / Knock / Ask Way 2 — Cross Way 3 — Tongues
Acceptable 1 of 3 of each of the 3 Seek and continue to seek Lay your life down Pray 1 hour in tongues
Good 2 of 3 of each of the 3 Seek and knock — and keep seeking and knocking Lay your life down and pick up the cross of submitting to someone else who is leading in front of you Pray 2 hours in Holy Spirit tongues
Perfect 3 of 3 of all 3 Seek and keep seeking, knock and keep knocking, ask and keep asking Do not let your soul have a say; put your life into the submission of someone who has done it before; replicate Jesus in the TWAMI Pray 3 hours speaking in Holy Spirit tongues

How the words are used here

  • Seek = read the Word of God on a topical search.
  • Knock = demand that God open the windows of Heaven.
  • Ask = declare over our life the same thing God has already declared.

Way 1 — Seek, knock, and ask

Jesus stacked these as a continuing command, not a one-time event. The Greek present imperative is keep on: keep asking, keep seeking, keep knocking.

Matthew 7:7–8 — Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you: For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened. (See also Luke 11:9–13.)

Acceptable will — 1 of 3 Seek and continue to seek

Open the Book on the subject God has put in front of you, and stay there until the Word has been searched. Seeking is not browsing for a verse that agrees with you; it is hunting what God actually said on the topic.

  • Jeremiah 29:13 — And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.
  • Proverbs 2:1–5 — Seek wisdom as silver, and search for her as for hid treasures.
  • Acts 17:11 — They searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.
  • 2 Timothy 2:15 — Study to shew thyself approved unto God… rightly dividing the word of truth.
  • Psalm 119:11, 18 — Thy word have I hid in mine heart… Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.

Good will — 2 of 3 Seek and knock, and continue both

After the Word has been sought, knock: insist that Heaven open what the Word has already named. Knocking is not unbelief; it is refusing to treat a closed sky as the last word when God has promised an open window.

  • Matthew 7:8 — To him that knocketh it shall be opened.
  • Luke 11:5–8 — Because of his importunity he will rise and give him as many as he needeth.
  • Malachi 3:10 — Prove me now herewith… if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing.
  • Isaiah 45:2–3 — I will go before thee, and make the crooked places straight… I will give thee the treasures of darkness.
  • Revelation 3:8 — I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it.

Perfect will — 3 of 3 Seek, knock, and ask — and keep on

Asking, in this walk, is not begging as if God had not spoken. It is declaring over your life the same thing God has already declared, until earth agrees with Heaven.

  • 1 John 5:14–15 — If we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us… we know that we have the petitions that we desired of him.
  • Isaiah 55:11 — My word… shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please.
  • Job 22:28 — Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways.
  • Mark 11:23–24 — Whosoever shall say unto this mountain… he shall have whatsoever he saith. What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye receive them.
  • Amos 3:7 — The Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets. (Ask what He has already shown.)

Way 2 — Lay down your life, pick up the cross, replicate Jesus

Jesus did not offer a self-directed spirituality. He named death to self, a cross carried under another’s lead, and a life copied from His.

Luke 9:23 — If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me. (See also Matthew 16:24; Mark 8:34.)

Acceptable will — 1 of 3 Lay your life down

The first requisite is surrender of the life itself — not a mood of willingness, but the actual placing of your life on the altar.

  • John 15:13 — Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends.
  • 1 John 3:16 — He laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.
  • Romans 12:1 — Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God.
  • Galatians 2:20 — I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me.
  • John 12:24–25 — Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone… He that loveth his life shall lose it.

Good will — 2 of 3 Lay your life down and pick up the cross of submitting to someone leading in front of you

The cross here is not private suffering you script for yourself. It is submitting your life to someone else who is leading in front of you — a living yoke, not a slogan.

  • Matthew 16:24 — Take up his cross, and follow me.
  • Hebrews 13:17 — Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls.
  • 1 Corinthians 11:1 — Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ.
  • 2 Timothy 2:2 — The things that thou hast heard of me… the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.
  • Matthew 11:29–30 — Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me… For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

Perfect will — 3 of 3 Soul silent; submitted to one who has done it; replicate Jesus in the TWAMI

Do not let your soul have a say. Put your life into the submission of someone who has already walked this. Replicate Jesus in the TWAMI — walk as He walked, not as the soul prefers.

  • Matthew 26:39 — Nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt.
  • Hebrews 4:12 — The word of God… dividing asunder of soul and spirit… and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
  • 1 Corinthians 9:27 — I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection.
  • Philippians 2:5–8 — Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus… he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.
  • 1 John 2:6 — He that saith he abideth in him ought himself also so to walk, even as he walked.
  • John 13:15 — I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.
  • Ephesians 5:1–2 — Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children; and walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us.

Way 3 — Pray in Holy Spirit tongues: one hour, two hours, three hours

Jesus named an hour in Gethsemane. The Spirit gives a language the mind does not author. Duration here is not a superstition; it is the measure of remaining with Him until the flesh yields.

Matthew 26:40–41 — What, could ye not watch with me one hour? Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

Acceptable will — 1 of 3 Pray one hour in tongues

Stay the hour. Pray with the Spirit in a tongue the Holy Spirit supplies.

  • Matthew 26:40 — Could ye not watch with me one hour?
  • 1 Corinthians 14:2 — He that speaketh in an unknown tongue speaketh not unto men, but unto God… in the spirit he speaketh mysteries.
  • 1 Corinthians 14:14–15 — If I pray in an unknown tongue, my spirit prayeth… I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also.
  • Acts 2:4 — They were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

Good will — 2 of 3 Pray two hours speaking in Holy Spirit tongues

Remain past the first hour. Build yourself up. Pray in the Spirit on all occasions — not as a slogan, as actual time given.

  • Jude 20 — Building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost.
  • Ephesians 6:18 — Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance.
  • Romans 8:26–27 — The Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered… he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.
  • Colossians 4:2 — Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving.

Perfect will — 3 of 3 Pray three hours speaking in Holy Spirit tongues

Three hours is the full measure in this pattern: the flesh has been outlasted, the Spirit has been given room, and the will of God is being prayed — not the soul’s agenda.

  • Luke 6:12 — He went out into a mountain to pray, and continued all night in prayer to God.
  • Luke 18:1 — Men ought always to pray, and not to faint.
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:17 — Pray without ceasing.
  • 1 Corinthians 14:18 — I thank my God, I speak with tongues more than ye all.
  • Acts 1:14 — These all continued with one accord in prayer and supplication.

How the three wills sit together

Romans 12 does not treat “acceptable,” “good,” and “perfect” as three rival gods. They are three depths of the same will, proved in a presented body and a renewed mind. One of three is still on the altar. Two of three is a cross under another’s lead plus a door you will not stop knocking. Three of three is Jesus copied: Word sought, Heaven knocked, God’s own decree asked; soul silenced under proven leadership; three hours in the Spirit until the flesh is no longer the clock.

Ephesians 5:17 — Wherefore be ye not unwise, but understanding what the will of the Lord is.
Colossians 1:9–10 — That ye might be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding… that ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing.
John 5:19, 30 — The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do… I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me.
