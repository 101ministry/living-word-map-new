3 Ways to Copy Jesus Flawlessly

The acceptable, good, and perfect will of God — three requisites, three levels

There is a reality called the perfect will of God. Another called the good will of God. Finally the acceptable will of God.

Within the perfect will, 3 of 3 requisites have been completed. Within the good will, 2 of 3. Within the acceptable will, 1 of 3.

The three requisites are themselves three-fold: seek, knock, and ask; lay your life down, pick up the cross, and follow / replicate Jesus; and pray one hour, two hours, or three hours in Holy Spirit tongues. To walk in a given will, complete that many steps of each of the three.

Romans 12:1–2

How the words are used here

  • Seek = read the Word of God on a topical search.
  • Knock = demand that God open the windows of Heaven.
  • Ask = declare over our life the same thing God has already declared.

Way 1 — Seek, knock, and ask

Jesus stacked these as a continuing command, not a one-time event. Luke records the same command as keep asking, keep seeking, keep knocking.

Matthew 7:7–8

7“Ask, and it will be given you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and it will be opened for you. 8For everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it will be opened.”

Luke 11:5–13

5He said to them, “Which of you, if you go to a friend at midnight, and tell him, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread, 6for a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him,’ 7and he from within will answer and say, ‘Don’t bother me. The door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed. I can’t get up and give it to you’? 8I tell you, although he will not rise and give it to him because he is his friend, yet because of his persistence, he will get up and give him as many as he needs. 9“I tell you, keep asking, and it will be given you. Keep seeking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened to you. 10For everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it will be opened. 11“Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, he won’t give him a snake instead of a fish, will he? 12Or if he asks for an egg, he won’t give him a scorpion, will he? 13If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?”

Acceptable will — 1 of 3 Seek and continue to seek

Open the Book on the subject God has put in front of you, and stay there until the Word has been searched. Seeking is not browsing for a verse that agrees with you; it is hunting what God actually said on the topic.

Good will — 2 of 3 Seek and knock, and continue both

After the Word has been sought, knock: insist that Heaven open what the Word has already named. Knocking is not unbelief; it is refusing to treat a closed sky as the last word when God has promised an open window. Matthew 7:7–8 and Luke 11:5–13 above already give the full knock command. These are the window-and-door passages.

Malachi 3:10

10“Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in my house, and test me now in this,” says Yahweh of Armies, “if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there will not be room enough for.”

Isaiah 45:2–3

2“I will go before you, and make the rough places smooth. I will break the doors of brass in pieces, and cut apart the bars of iron. 3I will give you the treasures of darkness, and hidden riches of secret places, that you may know that it is I, Yahweh, who call you by your name, even the God of Israel.”

Revelation 3:8

8“I know your works (behold, I have set before you an open door, which no one can shut), that you have a little power, and kept my word, and didn’t deny my name.”

Perfect will — 3 of 3 Seek, knock, and ask — and keep on

Asking, in this walk, is not begging as if God had not spoken. It is declaring over your life the same thing God has already declared, until earth agrees with Heaven.

Way 2 — Lay down your life, pick up the cross, replicate Jesus

Jesus did not offer a self-directed spirituality. He named death to self, a cross carried under another’s lead, and a life copied from His. The three Gospel listings of that command:

Luke 9:23

23He said to all, “If anyone desires to come after me, let him deny himself, take up his cross, and follow me.”

Matthew 16:24

24Then Jesus said to his disciples, “If anyone desires to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.”

Mark 8:34

34He called the multitude to himself with his disciples, and said to them, “Whoever wants to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.”

The first requisite is surrender of the life itself — not a mood of willingness, but the actual placing of your life on the altar.

Way 3 — Pray in Holy Spirit tongues: one hour, two hours, three hours

Jesus named an hour in Gethsemane. The Spirit gives a language the mind does not author. Duration here is not a superstition; it is the measure of remaining with Him until the flesh yields.

Matthew 26:40–41

40He came to the disciples, and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “What, couldn’t you watch with me for one hour? 41Watch and pray, that you don’t enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”

Acceptable will — 1 of 3 Pray one hour in tongues

Stay the hour. Pray with the Spirit in a tongue the Holy Spirit supplies. Matthew 26:40–41 is already printed in full above.

1 Corinthians 14:2

2For he who speaks in another language speaks not to men, but to God; for no one understands; but in the Spirit he speaks mysteries.

Acts 2:4

Remain past the first hour. Build yourself up. Pray in the Spirit on all occasions — not as a slogan, as actual time given.

How the three wills sit together

Romans 12 does not treat “acceptable,” “good,” and “perfect” as three rival gods. They are three depths of the same will, proved in a presented body and a renewed mind. One of three is still on the altar. Two of three is a cross under another’s lead plus a door you will not stop knocking. Three of three is Jesus copied: Word sought, Heaven knocked, God’s own decree asked; soul silenced under proven leadership; three hours in the Spirit until the flesh is no longer the clock.
