The Minions of the Spirit Spouse

9/23/2026 — Topics 442–573 counted against the succubus and incubus identities — Berean Standard Bible

This is the third sheet in the Spirit Spouse trio. The first named the lure (succubus). The second named the jailer (incubus). This one names the household: every identity from Topics 666 numbers 442 through 573, and how many of them show up in support of, or in league with, those two thrones.

In the chart, 442 is the last Destructive Attitude (having thoughts of anxiety). 443–573 are Destructive Identities Against God’s Image: familiar identities. Inside that range sit the two thrones themselves: 503. familiar identity of incubus and 561. familiar identity of succubus, both from a root of idolatry and person-worship. The rest of the range is the staff. A familiar identity is not a costume. It is a name the enemy trains a person to answer to, so the Spirit Spouse does not have to work alone.

Writing is kept PG. Myths are not a second Bible. Counts below are from Topics 666 as written: same worship-root as 503 and 561; name-echoes into Spirit Spouse Gods topics 574–666; and pattern-league with the succubus study (lure, drain, night, fantasy, addiction, vanity) and the incubus study (jealousy, control, dominance, possessiveness, cruelty). An identity may serve one throne or both. Scripture text: Berean Standard Bible (public domain).

VIII. WHAT THE COUNT IS SAYING

Out of 130 staff identities, more report to the incubus (105 including his own throne) than to the succubus (96 including hers). That is not because the lure is weaker. It is because the jailer uses a larger violence-and-control vocabulary on this chart, and because the shared household (fear, death, occult, shame) was counted toward both. The succubus still has twenty-six specialists whose whole job is the soft snare. The incubus has thirty-five specialists whose whole job is the chain. Sixty-nine keep the house running either way.

If a person only repents of "the succubus" or only of "the incubus," one hundred thirty other names in this range can still answer the door. The minions do not resign when the throne is named. They scatter into fantasy, jealousy, nightmares, witchcraft, and worry, and they wait.

IX. TALES FROM THE BIBLE

Scripture does not use the medieval words succubus and incubus. It does name turning to spiritists, prostituting oneself with them, inquiring of the dead, and servants who masquerade as light. The familiar identity is that masquerade worn as a self.

Leviticus 19:31

31You must not turn to mediums or spiritists; do not seek them out, or you will be defiled by them. I am the LORD your God.

Leviticus 20:6

6Whoever turns to mediums or spiritists to prostitute himself with them, I will also set My face against that person and cut him off from his people.

Leviticus 20:6 puts prostitution in the same sentence as spiritists. The succubus sheet already named the sale of the body. This verse names the sale of the soul to a familiar. The incubus sheet named ownership. This verse names God cutting off the one who sold himself.

Deuteronomy 18:9-12

9When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, do not imitate the detestable ways of the nations there. 10Let no one be found among you who sacrifices his son or daughter in the fire, practices divination or conjury, interprets omens, practices sorcery, 11casts spells, consults a medium or spiritist, or inquires of the dead. 12For whoever does these things is detestable to the LORD. And because of these detestable things, the LORD your God is driving out the nations before you.

2 Corinthians 11:14-15

14And no wonder, for Satan himself masquerades as an angel of light. 15It is not surprising, then, if his servants masquerade as servants of righteousness. Their end will correspond to their actions.

A familiar identity is a servant in costume. Vanity looks like beauty. Jealousy looks like protection. Fantasy looks like comfort. The end still corresponds to the actions.

Ephesians 6:12

12For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this world’s darkness, and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms.

1 John 4:1-3

1Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God. For many false prophets have gone out into the world. 2By this you will know the Spirit of God: Every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God, 3and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming and which is already in the world at this time.

Test the name you have been answering to. If it will not confess Jesus come in the flesh, it is not a personality quirk. It is a spirit. The minions fail that test on purpose.
