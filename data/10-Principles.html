10 Principles

A way to walk — not on common-sense logic, but toward Jesus

Christianity without Christ still uses His vocabulary while refusing His rule. It looks like solid ground. It is quicksand named common sense logic. Walking on water toward Jesus is the path that holds.

These ten principles are how to walk instead of sink: principalities not people, forgiveness that closes portals, a secret life that does not poison the Body, crowns of iniquity removed, soul ties burned, imagination given to God, time and position and thought sacrificed, and walking only by Holy Spirit.

1. Principalities are the problem, not people

The moment the world’s populace treats people as the source of the problem, principalities, regional familiar spirits, and bloodline familiar spirits know they are safe. The person who wrestles or argues against people is walking against the design of God.

If it were God’s design to go after people, Jesus would have done that in Matthew 16:23. He did not. He addressed what was speaking through Peter, not Peter as the enemy. If Jesus never went after the person, why do we?

2. Forgive the person for their actions, thoughts, words, how you remember what happened, and how they were influenced against you

This principle has five parts.

A. Forgive the actions.

a. Recognize that the hurt occurred. It may be a physical injury, an emotional injury, or trauma that grew because either one went unchecked. Healing begins when you admit you were actually hurt.

b. Once you have begun to recognize the hurt, speak about it only when you can say, “But I forgave this person.”

c. Walk in a lifestyle of repentance. Change the life patterns that put you in the time and place where that hurt could occur.

B. Forgive the person for their thoughts against you.

An action done in aggravation against another is not an accident. The wound was done with purpose or with ignorance, but it did not “just happen.” Significant thought, or a thought pattern, went into the physical or emotional wounding. You and I do not benefit by holding any unforgiveness toward our aggressors. An emotional wound is a portal for the kingdom of darkness to slip familiar spirits into your life, and they will cause chaos beyond the original wound.

C. Forgive the person for their words against you.

A saying was planted in my life as a teenager: “Hurting people hurt people.” Someone carrying baggage from the turnstile of hurts will pick that baggage up and walk in a way that wounds the people around them. We can ignore the fact that their words are hurtful, or we can forgive those words. Their words do not only pass through the physical ear gate. They pass through the soul and spirit gates as well. It is better to forgive and close the gates than to leave them open and stay contaminated.

D. Forgive the memories you have kept of that person.

I have taught this often in the Repentance101 livestream on YouTube, especially in the Trees of Bitterness series. Memories are often the worst part of a hurt, because an unforgiven hurt shows up in bloodlines as secondhand hurt and anger. Any memory of a hurt that is not given over to God has been shown, psychologically and medically, to trigger everything from stomach ulcers, muscle tension, and irritable bowel syndrome to various cancers. Those memories also widen the emotional wound so familiar spirits can enter, and they give those spirits permission to pass the same memories to your children and their children. Why carry that pain in your body when you could hand it to God?

E. Forgive the person for being influenced by the kingdom of darkness against you and against themselves.

This is the most powerful tool in the arsenal of forgiveness. Every person on earth lives on a battlefield of influence. We are being influenced by Holy Spirit, or we are being influenced by familiar spirits. Forgiving a person for being influenced by the kingdom of darkness against you and against themselves closes two emotional-wound portals with one prayer. We already battle enough with the kingdom of darkness going after everything we touch. If we forgive each other for that influence, we take a lot of fiery darts out of the equation (Ephesians 6:16).

3. What you do in your secret life affects other believers

For those who want to hide behind closed doors, an incognito browser, or disappearing conversations: everything you do is open to the Kingdom of God, and selected things are available to the kingdom of darkness.

I am not asking why you want to walk in secret. I am asking this: why do you hate your brother or sister so much that a poisoned action, a poisoned incognito habit, or a poisoned disappearing conversation becomes the point of contact for the kingdom of darkness to go after you and the person you are trying to hide from?

I am not talking about legitimate secrecy required for government work so an enemy cannot catch you. I am talking about a secret fling, a secret browsing addiction, or a secret conversation where you premeditate demonic plans against another person. All of those actions sit like a supermarket for the kingdom of darkness, which will shop for a convincing “good reason” for what you do.

If your actions are not in alignment with how Jesus taught His disciples to walk, and if they are not in alignment with the epistles written after the four Gospels, then your secret thing is a stumbling block for everyone around you. Worst of all, you become an ally of the kingdom of darkness: a wolf in sheep’s clothing.

4. Ask God to remove the crown of iniquities

Strongholds matter, but so do the thoughts that linger in the mind against the design of God. Those lingering thoughts become a crown of transgression. If that thinking is not given over to God, it becomes fodder for the familiar spirit and is passed to the next generation as iniquity. That iniquity can become the foundation for something like the Spirit of Python to clamp down the moment you desire to live for Christ.

A crown of iniquity is what keeps many people in bondage while they are honestly trying to live for Christ and walk out deliverance, whether with a deliverance minister or through self-deliverance.

5. Ask Holy Spirit to burn all soul ties, every day

One of the worst struggles a person can live with is having every event tied to every memory of every person they have ever met. Do you really want to remember every person according to your own perspective?

A human perspective is a pawn’s perspective. It is also filtered through territorial familiar spirits and through each person’s bloodline familiar spirits. That is where the mixing happens.

A soul tie is made when you encounter another person and lock in a first impression of what they are. Every later encounter cements that picture. As long as your way of seeing them is more important than how God sees them, your understanding has become an idol. That is not worth it. Idolatry will not get you into the Kingdom of Heaven, no matter how “saved” you think you are.

The good news is this: asking Holy Spirit to burn every soul tie, every day, is the easiest and godliest way to walk this life. Then you can see as Jesus sees, and walk as Jesus walked on earth and still walks in our lives.

6. The imagination is for God’s dreams and visions

This was never part of the original ten, but it has to be named. It is ravaging the youth of the nations, and it has a hook in every bloodline.

Fantasies, riveting imaginations, works of fiction, sordid tales, romance novels, anime, hentai, and other visual arts have one thing in common: they highlight the spirit spouse. This is a struggle for every person I have ever met, and for many I have not yet met. The lie is that life is about connection with another soul, whether that is called a soul mate or something else.

Any imagination that is not captured and brought into obedience to Jesus Christ is an image that defies the Lord Most High. If your imagination is anything other than how God says life is, you are serving evil. This one will be next to impossible unless the remaining four are in place.

7. Sacrifice of time

Time matters to God. How you spend time on earth, and how much of it you give to any one thing, measures how much worship you are giving that person, place, or thing. God is not a place or a thing. He is a Person.

If you truly want to worship and honor Him, reading the Bible daily is not what arrests His attention by itself. What arrests His attention is this: every time Holy Spirit reminds you of an action you or your ancestors have done, you agree immediately and begin to intercede for each person connected to your bloodline, and for every country connected to you by your physical DNA.

When your own clock no longer matters most, and the godly freedom of others matters more than your wants, Heaven moves.

8. Sacrifice of position

Personal opinions, personally held beliefs, and personal ideas of what it means to be a good person count as gold in the kingdom of darkness. Anything worth a lot to that kingdom is dung to the Kingdom of God.

Spiritual authority from the Throne Room of Heaven is based on how much you do not know. It cannot come from what you have learned. It must come from what Holy Spirit has told you.

If life is about how much you know in the spirit, how much you can recite from memory, or how often you say, “I understand things like this,” then you or someone near you has set a personalized understanding higher than God Almighty. Bloodline repentance is about making sure you do not look like the lofty position of the principalities. Your position is in Christ and nowhere else.

9. Sacrifice of thought

This is closely related to position. Holy Spirit commands us to bring every thought into captivity to Jesus Christ (2 Corinthians 10:5).

The primary way familiar spirits attack every human is through suggestive thought: questions, suggestions, dreams, or what others have said or are saying about you. Everything starts in the spirit realm. Next it becomes a thought. Then the thought is placed in a person’s mind. Each person, especially a disciple of Christ, must take that thought captive.

What does that mean? Ask whether the topic of the thought is something you can find in the Bible as something God said, would have said, or would say to Jesus. If it is none of those three, give that thought to God for Him to deal with. If the thought comes as a dream, wake up and cancel it.

Every thought is an assignment. Make sure you are not being assigned in a way that counters the things of God.

10. Walk only by the Spirit of God

This can sound like a repeat of the last three. It is not. In all the times you have tried to walk out repentance, did your soul and spirit get along? Did they play nice? When you were around others, did what they said have an impact that pulled you off course?

Unless Holy Spirit is the only guidance in your life, everything around you is another spark trying to set you off. The only fire you want burning in you is Holy Spirit fire. That fire is ignited when He leads and you follow.

The first nine are impossible if you do not walk by the Spirit of God only.

Abbreviated

I have discovered a way to walk and it involves the following:

  1. Principalities and spiritual beings are ALWAYS the problem. It is never the person.
  2. Forgiveness means recognizing a hurt happened / boundary was crossed. Forgiveness is giving the vengeance over to God by releasing the action, premeditated thought behind the action, the words, the memory of how it happened or how many times it happened, and how the person was influenced by the kingdom of darkness against you and against themselves.
  3. What you do in your secret life (behind closed doors or in your own thoughts and ideas) will affect every other person around you.
  4. Ask Holy Spirit to burn all soul ties.
  5. Ask Holy Spirit to remove all crowns of iniquity. To miss the mark of being holy before God = sin. To consistently miss the mark = transgression. To have your life bent in a particular habit to the point you cannot break free outside of the work of Holy Spirit = crown of iniquity, because it controls the way you think.
  6. God gave us imagination for dreams and visions. Any other way our imagination is used outside of God’s Design reflects a crown of iniquity.
  7. Give God 10% of the time that you spend doing everything else, every week. There are 168 hours in a week. 16.8 hours is 10%.
  8. Give God the position He is. Anything that I value more than God = idolatry. Anything I love more than Him = adultery. When I want my own way instead of His = rebellion. His way must be the top most important.
  9. Give God control of your thoughts. People everywhere will mess with me and I will want to give them a piece of my mind. But I am commanded by God to give Him ALL of my thoughts so that He can direct my path. If I trust in myself, lean on my abilities, and point to my strengths as the source for all of the things I have accomplished in life, I have made a mockery of Proverbs 3:5–6. He will not direct my path if I do not give Him my thoughts.
  10. Walk ONLY by Holy Spirit influence. This is how all the disciples were able to make it in their world, it is how Jesus did EVERYTHING in His time on earth, and it is how we are commanded to do. But, will we?
