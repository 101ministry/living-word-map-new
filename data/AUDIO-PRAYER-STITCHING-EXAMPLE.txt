LIVING WORD MAP — AUDIO PRAYER STITCHING (EXAMPLE)
==================================================

This file explains shells vs inserts, and shows how Topic 001 (Anger)
would be assembled for Round 1. Use it to reset how you think about
recording and building prayer audio.


WHAT IS A SHELL?
----------------

A SHELL is a fixed MP3 clip — words that are the SAME in every topic
prayer. Record each shell once. Reuse it in all 666 prayers.

An INSERT is a small MP3 clip that CHANGES per topic — the topic name,
root name, fruit/theme line, principality name, etc. Record one clip
per unique word/phrase in that category.


THE FORM-LETTER IDEA
--------------------

  [shell] + [insert] + [insert] + [shell] + [shell] + … → full prayer MP3

You are not recording 666 full prayers from scratch. You are recording:

  • ~8 shell clips (Round 1)
  • ~666 topic inserts
  • ~20–40 root inserts (however many distinct roots exist)
  • (+ fruit and principality inserts when a round needs them)

Then a script (e.g. Python) stitches them in order and saves one file
per topic, e.g.  output/round1/001-anger.mp3

ROUND 1 — STITCH ORDER FOR TOPIC 001 (ANGER)
--------------------------------------------

Written prayer (from compiled Round 1 source):

  001. PLEASE NOTE: THESE PRAYERS ARE TO BE SPOKEN, NOT SIMPLY READ SILENTLY.

  I agree that I am guilty of keeping and not casting down thought suggestions
  of anger, from a root of bitterness and unforgiveness.

  I agree that I made it more important than God, loved it more than God,
  and preferred it instead of God. For this idolatry, adultery, and rebellion,
  I lay my life on the altar of God.

  I recognize that anger, from a root of bitterness and unforgiveness; is
  happening because of agreements, blood covenants, blood contracts, hexes,
  vexes, interaction with the blood, and satanic ritual agreements because of
  Anger and Violence with the parent Principality of Jealousy

  For the accusation of this record, I lay my life down on my mother's side
  (say her full name), and my father's side (say his full name), all the
  way back to Adam.

  … (forgiveness block) …

  I no longer want to serve anger. In fact, I am asking for the forgiveness
  of God on this and for the Blood of Jesus to cover the record and speak
  instead. … In the name of Jesus, Amen

NOTE A — FRUIT + PRINCIPALITY (FULL TEXT VS SIMPLIFIED ROUND 1)
---------------------------------------------------------------

The written prayer after "agreements…" also says:

  "because of Anger and Violence with the parent Principality of Jealousy"

That part is NOT the same for every topic. Options:

  (1) SIMPLIFIED ROUND 1 AUDIO
      Bake a generic agreements shell and skip fruit/principality for now.
      Easier to start; text on screen still shows the full prayer.

  (2) FULL FIDELITY
      After the agreements shell, add inserts:
        fruits/anger-and-violence.mp3
        principalities/jealousy.mp3
      (exact wording must match your recorded clips)

  Round 2 and Round 3 may add more shells and more inserts. Same clip
  library — different stitch recipe per round.


THREE ROUNDS
------------

  Round 1   Foundation (recipe above). Fewest shells.
  Round 2   Adds some sections, removes or replaces others → new recipe.
  Round 3   Fullest version → third recipe.

  Record shells and inserts ONCE. Run the stitch script once per round:

    python stitch_prayer.py --round 1 --topic 001
    python stitch_prayer.py --round 2 --topic 001
    python stitch_prayer.py --round 3 --topic 001

  Output:
    output/round1/001-anger.mp3
    output/round2/001-anger.mp3
    output/round3/001-anger.mp3
