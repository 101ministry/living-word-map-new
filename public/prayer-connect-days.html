How the Prayers Connect

Each day writes out the Berean Standard Bible for one prayer heading (Days 1–16), then one day for each book of the Bible (Days 17–82). These prayers are to be spoken, not simply read silently.

“Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.” — Matthew 4:17 (BSB)

  1. Day 01 — I. How the prayers connect
  2. Day 02 — On behalf of the (2nd–5th / 6th–20th) generation… I agree WE are guilty.
  3. Day 03 — I agree that WE made it more important than God, loved it more than God, and preferred it instead of God.
  4. Day 04 — For this idolatry, adultery, and rebellion, I lay my life / my bloodline on the altar of God.
  5. Day 05 — I recognize that this is happening because of agreements, blood covenants, blood contracts, hexes, vexes, interaction with the blood, and satanic ritual agreements… because of [fruit] with the parent [Principality].
  6. Day 06 — For the accusation of this record, I lay down the 2nd–5th / 6th–20th generations (Round 1: mother's side, father's side, all the way back to Adam).
  7. Day 07 — I refuse to blame anyone anymore… I take accountability for what my bloodline copied… AND FORGIVE everyone who taught us.
  8. Day 08 — If people from other bloodlines have repented and we don't know about it, I STILL… forgive every way we remembered people the way Jesus doesn't.
  9. Day 09 — Representing my bloodline, I forgive them for not capturing every vain imagination and bringing it to Jesus as given in 2 Corinthians 10:3-5.
  10. Day 10 — …and not warring against people as given in Ephesians 6:12.
  11. Day 11 — I ask You Father to forgive my bloodline since I have forgiven other bloodlines. I ask You to judge between me and them so that the judgment is pure and holy.
  12. Day 12 — I no longer want to serve [topic] or [fruit]. I am asking for the forgiveness of God on this and for the Blood of Jesus to cover the record and speak instead.
  13. Day 13 — I also ask for a spiritual decree of divorce from this record. We don't have room in my bloodline for unconfessed records.
  14. Day 14 — I cast off of my head the crown of iniquity that had been attached to my head. You are banned from my life, my mind, my heart, and my body.
  15. Day 15 — Give me courage to be responsible for every other temptation and accusation… so that he no longer has any more open accusations against me. I thank You for the people You have put in my life to help me walk out a life of repentance, because of walking in discipleship.
  16. Day 16 — In the name of Jesus, Amen.
