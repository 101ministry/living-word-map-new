Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Living Word Map (map.repentance101.com) is a ministry site of Repentance101. This page explains what we collect when you use the map, prayers, teaching videos, and downloads.

Who we are

Repentance101 Ministries operates this site. Community and direct conversation live at repentance101ministry.com.

What we collect

Most map browsing, prayer reading, and PDF downloads do not require an account. Your device may store a theme preference and prayer-language choice in local browser storage so the page looks the same on the next visit.

If you enter the Prayer Builder / experimental People map, you give a case name, a place (city, region, country), a password, and (when used) an invite code. We keep a session so only people who came through that gate can see shared map regions. We do not ask for a government ID. Do not use a password you use for email or banking.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not use this site to run advertising profiles. Prayer answers you speak are between you and the Lord; we do not record your microphone from these pages.

Third parties

Teaching videos are hosted on YouTube. If you play a video, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies. The site is served through Cloudflare. Book links may send you to Amazon. Wispr Flow and other tools mentioned in descriptions are separate services with their own policies.

Children

This ministry content is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an experimental map session for a child under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: repentance101ministry.com or repentance101ministry.admin@gmail.com.
